02Practice

Every voice gets its own mix. In each mix one voice sits in the middle, the others are spread across the stereo image like the choir stands. You pick a voice, a stage, a sound and a tempo. The headphones show the distribution, and you hear exactly that mix. When the files are created you choose the sound: MuseScore General or GeneralUser GS. It sounds best on headphones. The recordings are mechanically exact, without interpretation.

04Rights & AI

Rights

Before scores are processed, the skill asks about the rights. These details are then recorded in the README file. Whether the scores may be processed and passed on depends on the copyright and licence law they are subject to.

AI labelling

Everything the skill creates is labelled as AI content, both with the EU label for AI-generated content and in the text and metadata of the documents.
