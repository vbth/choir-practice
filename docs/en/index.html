02Practice

Every voice gets its own mix. Your own voice sits in the middle, the others are spread across the stereo image like the choir stands. Pick a voice, a stage, a sound and a tempo: the headphones show the distribution, and you hear exactly that mix. It sounds best on headphones. The recordings are mechanically exact, without interpretation.

04Rights and AI

Rights

Before scores are processed, the skill asks about the rights. It records the answer in the pack’s README. The licences of the score must be observed.

Labelling

Everything the skill creates is labelled as AI content, both with the EU label for AI-generated content and in the text and metadata of the documents.

05Get started

  1. Upload choir-practice.skill in your Claude account (settings).
  2. Call the skill without a score and answer stop A (rights and intended use).
  3. Attach the score and answer stop B (work data).
  4. Download the ZIP at the end and pass the audio files on to the choir.
